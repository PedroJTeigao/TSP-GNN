## Summary

Reviewed by hand after the runs (the automatic draft is kept in the appendix; its control verdict was too coarse).

1. **Helped most, without retraining: denser test graphs (`kfrac`, k = 0.5 n).** Evaluating the phase 1 models with k = 25 / 50 instead of 10 is significantly better in 6/6 TSP-100 cells and 3/6 TSP-50 cells (all greedy; mean −1.5 pp on TSP-50, −4.0 pp on TSP-100). Cost (Gated GCN): inference + greedy goes from 3.8 to 6.1 ms per TSP-100 instance. With k fixed, each city sees half of the others in TSP-20 but only 1/5 in TSP-50, so node degrees and neighbourhoods at test time differ from training.
2. **Did not help much: distance normalization (`knn_mean`).** Only 2/18 cells better (TSP-100), and it hurts GATv2 on TSP-20/50. On top of `kfrac` it adds nothing (`knn_mean_kfrac` ≈ `kfrac`). The 0.58× distance-scale shift is real but is not the main cause of the failure.
3. **Helped partly: BatchNorm with test-batch statistics (`bn_batch`).** Better in 5/6 TSP-100 cells and for GATv2 greedy on TSP-50, unchanged on TSP-20 (GCN greedy slightly worse). So the stored BatchNorm statistics are part of the problem, but a smaller part than k. Caveat: it is transductive (each prediction depends on the other 255 test graphs of its batch).
4. **LayerNorm alone (k = 10) is a trade-off.** It is worse in-distribution (TSP-20 greedy: GATv2 +2.0 pp, Gated GCN +1.2 pp, both significant), not significantly different on TSP-50, and better on TSP-100 (4/6 cells).
5. **Best TSP-20-trained configuration: LayerNorm + k = 0.5 n.** It is significantly better than phase 1 in 5/6 TSP-50 and 6/6 TSP-100 cells (TSP-50 greedy: GCN 11.39, GATv2 11.27, Gated GCN 10.47 %, vs. 15.91 / 14.68 / 16.38 % in phase 1).
6. **Caveat on the LayerNorm results: model selection picked barely trained models.** With LayerNorm the validation loss keeps decreasing for all 20 epochs, but the greedy validation gap is lowest at epoch 1 for Gated GCN seeds 0 and 1 and at epoch 3 for GATv2 seeds 0 and 1. Part of their TSP-50/100 advantage may therefore come from under-fitting TSP-20. The Gated GCN seed selected at epoch 18 gives 12.78 % on TSP-50 with k = 0.5 n, and the seed spread is large (Gated GCN, TSP-100 with k = 10: 18.03 ± 3.70 %). This must be checked before relying on point 5.
7. **Control: training on TSP-50 fixes TSP-50.** The greedy gap on TSP-50 falls to 14.32 / 12.59 / 11.39 % (GCN / GATv2 / Gated GCN; Gated GCN from 16.38 %, with std 0.02 across seeds). The model ranking is the same as on TSP-20 (Gated > GATv2 > GCN). The models also generalize better to TSP-100 (all 6 cells significantly better than phase 1).
8. **Generalization or capacity? Mostly generalization, with a residual capacity/difficulty limit.** Models trained only on TSP-20 (LayerNorm + k = 0.5 n) match or beat the models trained on TSP-50 on TSP-50, so most of the phase 1 failure is a train/test mismatch (graph density, normalization statistics) and not a lack of capacity. But even trained in-distribution, the models reduce the classical-greedy gap by only 14–32 % on TSP-50, against 33–62 % on TSP-20: n = 50 is harder for H = 64, L = 6, 20 epochs. The control used k = 10 fixed (sparser at n = 50), so it may underestimate what in-distribution training can reach.
9. **With 2-opt the models now beat the classical baselines numerically.** On TSP-50: 2.96 % (Gated GCN, trained on 50) and 3.02 % (GATv2, LayerNorm + k = 0.5 n), vs. 3.66 % (greedy + 2-opt) and 3.63 % (Christofides + 2-opt). On TSP-100: 3.73 % vs. 4.29 %. In phase 1 they were equal (GCN, 3.66 %) or worse (GATv2 4.07 %, Gated GCN 4.78 %) on TSP-50. These comparisons with the baselines were not tested statistically in this report.
10. **Suggested next checks:** select the LayerNorm models by validation loss (or take the last epoch) to remove the epoch-1 effect; train the TSP-50 control with k = 0.5 n; run paired tests against the baselines for the best configurations.

## Decisions

Choices made without asking (the most conservative option each time):

1. `k_frac`: k = min(n − 1, round(k_frac × n)); for n = 20, 50, 100 and k_frac = 0.5 this is exactly 10, 25, 50. Without `--k-frac` the fixed k = 10 of phase 1 is used (bit-for-bit identical results, checked with `array_equal` on copies of the phase 1 and experiment 1 models).
2. Experiment 2 evaluates only on TSP-50 and TSP-100, as requested: on TSP-20, k = 0.5 × 20 = 10 is the phase 1 graph (a unit test checks the graphs are identical), so those cells are left as "—". For the LayerNorm models (experiment 3) the k = 0.5 n evaluation also includes TSP-20, as requested there; it must equal the k = 10 value.
3. `knn_mean` combined with k = 0.5 n divides by the mean edge length of the *larger* k-NN graph, i.e. the definition of knn_mean applied literally to the test graph (not the k = 10 mean seen in training).
4. Evaluations with k = 0.5 n use 64 graphs per forward pass instead of 256 (peak GPU memory 0.7 GB instead of 2.8 GB on the shared GPU). In eval mode the result does not depend on the batch size.
5. `--bn-batch-stats`: the BatchNorms of a *copy* of the model drop the stored running statistics, so each layer normalizes with the mean/variance of the current test batch: 256 graphs of the same size (the last batch has 232 graphs on TSP-20 and 244 on TSP-50/100). This makes the prediction for one instance depend on the other instances of its batch (transductive); per-instance statistics (batch of 1 graph) were not tried. Weights and files are not modified.
6. LayerNorm (experiment 3) replaces every BatchNorm of the encoder (the per-layer norms of GCN/GATv2 and both norms of each Gated GCN layer) with `nn.LayerNorm(64)` (PyTorch defaults); nothing else changes (raw distances, k = 10, same seeds, epochs, learning rate and model selection on TSP-20 validation).
7. Control: new data `tsp50_train` (12000 instances, seed 6) and `tsp50_val` (500, seed 7), LKH with the phase 1 settings. Model selection uses `tsp50_val` (in-distribution, as phase 1 used `tsp20_val`). Same epochs, batch size (64 graphs, so 2.5× more edges per batch) and learning rate; pos_weight is recomputed from the TSP-50 data. Evaluated on TSP-50 and TSP-100 only, as requested.
8. Phase 1 TSP-100 results are the ones computed in experiment 1 (`results/phase2_exp1/phase1_tsp100/`); they were not recomputed.
9. Paired comparisons pair seed s of a configuration with seed s of phase 1. For kfrac and bn_batch this is the same trained network; for retrained configurations the seed pairing is arbitrary and the instances are what is paired. The Bonferroni correction is applied over *all* tests in Table 2 (stricter than per experiment).
10. Conservative reading of the rules: nothing from phase 1 or experiment 1 was rewritten (new folders only); a failed step is logged and the steps that depend on it are skipped; `sqrt_n` was not trained; the report is copied to `docs/` but not committed.
11. The **Summary** was rewritten by hand after reading the results. The automatic rule ("≥ 75 % of the TSP-20 advantage recovered") concluded "capacity" and ignored that TSP-20-trained LayerNorm + k = 0.5 n models match the TSP-50-trained ones. The automatic draft is in the appendix. Re-running `scripts/phase2_batch2_report.py` regenerates only the automatic version and would overwrite this file.
12. The run finished with 0 failed steps (`results/phase2_batch2.log`, 09:34 → 11:15 on 2026-10-18).
